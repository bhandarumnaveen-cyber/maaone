Every day, small service businesses across India lose customers for the simplest reason: **the reply came too late.**

An enquiry comes in on the website while you're on a job. A WhatsApp message arrives while you're with another customer. By the time you sit down to reply — and find the right words — the customer has already booked someone else.

Maa One exists to fix that first, and then to run the rest of your business from the same app.

## What ships first: the Maa One Inbox (v1, at launch)

- **One inbox** for your website enquiries and WhatsApp chats.
- **AI lead scoring** so you know who is ready to buy and who needs a follow-up.
- **AI reply drafts in Hindi, Punjabi or English** — including Hinglish, the way your customers actually write. You read it, tap approve, and it's sent.
- **A simple CRM**, lead widgets for any website, and an app you can install on any phone.

## What comes next

We'll add a new module every two to three months and small improvements every two weeks:

- **v1.5 (soon after launch):** the Android app, then iOS.
- **v2 (next):** bookings, quotes, GST invoices and UPI payment links.
- **v3 (later):** a technician app, AMC renewals, Google reviews and WhatsApp campaigns.
- **v4 (future):** khata, expenses, the "Ask Maa" assistant, agency mode and an API.

You can see every date on the [roadmap](/roadmap).

## One fair price per business
